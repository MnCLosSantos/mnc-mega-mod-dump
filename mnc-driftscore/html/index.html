How Scoring Works

  • Minimum drift angle: 15°
  • Spin-out (excessive angle/rotation) or collision = chain reset (chain is lost, no Reputation earned)
  • Chain ends after 2 seconds without drifting — if it isn't lost to a reset, it's saved into your total
  • Many different combos stack multipliers (angle, speed, proximity, duration...)
  • Advanced combos: Tandem, Reverse Entry, Clutch Kick, Chicane, Donut, E-Brake, Link Drift
  • Style changes are saved between sessions
  • Your best score is tracked on the Weekly, Monthly and All-Time leaderboards

Reputation & Drift Coins

  • Every saved chain earns Reputation — 100 chain points = 1 Reputation
  • Every 10 Reputation also earns you 1 Drift Coin
  • Reputation and Drift Coins are persistent and always visible in the HUD and on /driftboard
  • Spend Drift Coins in the Store tab on /driftboard for items and bank rewards
